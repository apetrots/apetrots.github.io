**Hey there! I’m Zach.** I'm a 23 year old maker who just graduated with my Bachelor's in Electrical Engineering. I've always enjoyed making things with my hands and my imagination. Over the years I’ve tried a little bit of everything—game development, tinkering with electronics, delving into amateur radio and signal processing, carving wooden art and crafting furniture, backpacking and mycology. I'm constantly curious and **I love learning** new things! I love sharing that knowledge, too, hence this page.

I don't always know where a project will end up, but I love the process of learning and figuring it out as I go. This site is just a place to share that journey. Some of what you’ll see here is finished, some of it’s still in progress, and some of it might just be ideas. Either way, it’s all part of me exploring, experimenting, and hopefully getting a little better each time.

**If you're here for my portfolio,** I recommend looking through my [resume](/resume/) page and [projects](/projects/) page!
